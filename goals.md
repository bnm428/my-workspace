# H2 2026 Work Goals

---

## Goal 1: Achieve ≥95% On-Time Delivery by Q4 2026

Improve production schedule adherence from current baseline to a sustained on-time delivery (OTD) rate of 95% or higher across all active work orders by December 31, 2026. Progress will be tracked monthly via the program metrics dashboard and reviewed in weekly production readiness meetings. Root cause analysis will be performed on any month falling below target, with corrective actions documented in the action item log.

---

## Goal 2: Reduce Production Defect Escape Rate to <2% by December 2026

Drive quality improvement on the three highest-risk product lines by implementing enhanced first-article inspection (FAI) checkpoints and updating inspection travelers by August 1, 2026. Target a defect escape rate below 2% as measured by internal quality audits and customer rejection data. Report progress quarterly to the Quality Management Review board and close out all open Corrective Action / Preventive Action (CAPA) items by November 30, 2026.

---

## Goal 3: Cross-Train 80% of Production Technicians Across ≥2 Skill Classifications by November 2026

Develop and execute a workforce cross-training plan that qualifies at least 80% of direct labor technicians in a minimum of two skill classifications by November 30, 2026. This reduces single-point-of-failure risk, improves schedule flexibility during absences or surges, and supports succession readiness. Training completion and qualification records will be maintained in the skills matrix and reviewed at monthly one-on-ones with each team lead.
